## Original-contract trace

This directly supplies the low-end evidence requested by `OC-025`, `OC-050`, `OC-051`, and `OC-064`.
The original journal selection had to meet `< 1 ms p95`, and the plan explicitly warned that a latency
harness deferred until after selection would be incapable of changing the design in time.

## Construction and state-machine effect

The selected journal bytes do not change. A small server bundles the same specification module used by
Playwright, serves it with COOP/COEP headers over a trustworthy origin, checks observed clock resolution,
and accepts device results with user agent and isolation state into ignored `test-results/latency.jsonl`.
The page measures four cumulative levels at 18, 419, and 4,096 bytes, each with 30 dynamically sized
batches and 4,000 individually timed operations. It repeats the 18 B and 4 kB seal at the end to detect
thermal/order drift.

The resulting decision is deliberately not a construction change: 145 and 295 microseconds p95 are
inside 1 ms for the common/paste inputs, while 1,930 microseconds at 4 kB breaches it. Stage 1 must fix
or consciously revise the payload-qualified budget.

## Assumptions and guarantee boundaries

- The numbers are observations from a Pixel 9a on 2026-08-19. The device is not itself a lower bound
  for every browser/device allowed by the support floor, so passing common cases cannot be generalized.
- The benchmark runs a specification-built microbenchmark outside React/Yjs/IndexedDB and does not
  establish integrated crash ordering or contention.
- It repeatedly overwrites one warmed localStorage key rather than appending immutable production-shaped
  entries, omitting storage-growth and new-key effects.
- Cross-origin isolation and measured clock resolution reduce quantization; they do not make the 4,000
  adjacent operations independent workload samples.
- The two-run 1.4% reproduction and raw samples are recorded only in prose/ignored result files; the exact
  historical device/browser session is not reproducible from Git.

## Evidence and oracle independence

The pre-measurement vector check protects the selected primitive/framing, and the hardware result is
independent of the earlier desktop-throttle extrapolation it falsifies. The runner, summary arithmetic,
and construction remain project-owned. The device label, date, code, locked dependencies, sample counts,
clock check, and summary values survive; raw per-operation samples, OS/browser version, battery/thermal
state, and the second run do not.

The arithmetic is sound: 1,930 microseconds is 1.93 times the 1 ms budget; 1,498.3/265.7 is about 5.6;
and the reported seal/native-path device-to-desktop ratios follow the table. The 7%/2% repeated-seal
drift is below the chosen 15% warning threshold, though it does not repeat every stored level.

## Consequences and residual failures

The 4 kB result decisively falsifies the earlier claim that desktop headroom safely predicts this
phone. Keeping the budget and recording a defect is the honest course while payload policy is open.
The result does not establish a universal low-end pass at smaller sizes, and the fixed-slot harness is not
the immutable journal write the prose names. A missing/corrupt plaintext `1:` migration rule, integrated
failure behavior, and large-update policy remain explicitly deferred.

## Falsification attempt and counterevidence

- I recomputed all headline ratios and budget comparisons; they agree within rounding.
- I checked the drift implementation: it repeats the earliest and latest **seal** levels after the full
  ordered run, supporting the payload effect but not directly retesting storage/framing levels.
- I challenged the emulation inference. Non-uniform ratios across cipher and native paths on the same
  device directly refute one scalar throttle as a conservative model.
- I compared the measured storage operation with the specified journal. The harness uses one fixed,
  pre-warmed slot for thousands of replacements, while production is immutable and accumulating.
- I searched the commit for raw results. The receiver records them under ignored `test-results`; only
  summary prose is durable.

## Nuance preservation

The commit preserves that the budget may eventually be revised, 4 kB's product relevance is open, one
phone is evidence rather than a universal model, and a breach should not silently change the selected
nonce/format. It overstates “whole write” and “budget holds” where the fixed-slot microbenchmark and
support-floor coverage stop.

## Verdict

`supported-with-qualifications`: the device run validly falsifies the desktop/throttle extrapolation and
demonstrates a 4 kB breach on the recorded Pixel 9a. It does not establish production-shaped localStorage
cost or a support-floor-wide pass at smaller inputs.
