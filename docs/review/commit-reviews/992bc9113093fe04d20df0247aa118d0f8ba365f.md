## Construction and state-machine effect

### Envelope

The clear header carries version, workspace, credential, epoch, generation, credential position, 12-byte nonce, and
SHA-256 ciphertext digest. AES-256-GCM seals one actor and a length-framed update batch under a workspace-separated
network subkey; its AAD binds all non-circular header fields. The source signature binds the same identity/position
fields plus nonce and digest, reaching values that structurally cannot be AEAD AAD. One `seq` is both client-minted
outbound position and accepted-chain position, allowing every receiver to replay the chain without a service-assigned
index. Batches close on actor/credential/epoch/generation change; interval and byte cap remain measured tunables.

### Head acquisition

Enrollment carries the epoch head through the person by adding it to the committed SAS transcript. Migration/archive
is specified to carry a signed head in the package. Only total-loss recovery asks the authorization service: the
arriving device sends 32 fresh bytes and accepts a `K_auth` signature over workspace, epoch/head, its exact nonce and
display-only issue time, then persists the first head so ordinary ancestor rejection applies thereafter.

## Assumptions and guarantee boundaries

- The relay sees credential, epoch, generation, position, traffic sizes/timing, nonce and digest; actor remains sealed.
- Capability is not an envelope field; a receiver must resolve credential+epoch and check the decrypted actor/action.
- AES-GCM nonce randomness is caller-supplied; `seq` is a conservative count only if re-mints at one position are
  separately bounded/accounted.
- A client cannot skip a refused position. Safe same-position re-minting is true only after later commit `745e332`
  limits it to authoritative definite refusal; silence/timeout must wait.
- Enrollment resists service head rollback only when the existing device has a trusted current head and the person
  completes transcription. Package arrival depends on the package's independent anchor.
- Total-loss assertion stops path replay but deliberately cannot stop a hostile authorization service choosing a
  stale self-consistent first view; the result is a partition/availability bound, not global freshness.

## Evidence and oracle independence

The envelope generator checks four compositions and 21 negatives spanning AAD, signature, subkey, ciphertext/tag,
digest, and signer. The head generator checks the signed assertion and its fields; the amended enrollment generator
checks stale-head transcript disagreement. Exact `--check` execution of all three passed in a disposable `992bc91`
snapshot on 2026-08-21.

These are project-owned composition oracles built on row-1 WebCrypto primitives. They strongly pin encodings and
isolated bindings but do not exercise authorization lookup, actor/capability validation, relay persistence,
accepted-head cadence, integrated batching, or user transcription. The head vector correctly describes its residual
rather than treating a `K_auth` signature as an independent freshness oracle against `K_auth`'s own service.

## Consequences and residual failures

The two-list binding design authentically reaches every clear field without circular AEAD inputs; one client-owned
position is necessary for independently replayed accepted chains; one-actor batches preserve the selected
attribution granularity. Head acquisition avoids unnecessary service trust for enrollment and attempts to do so for
packages, while accurately reducing total-loss recovery to first-contact service trust.

At this commit, the claim that a refused same-position re-mint cannot look like equivocation is too broad: only the
later definite-refusal rule makes it true. The package path is also only a promise here and later receives the flawed
root anchor recorded in `f-745e332-package-root-rewrap-substitution`. Within-epoch stale content remains possible at
first contact and is part of the disclosed partition residual. No integrated implementation exists at the pinned tip.

## Falsification attempt and counterevidence

- I reran all three exact generators; every published negative still failed.
- I traced nonce and digest across both authenticity layers: nonce is the AEAD IV and signature-bound; ciphertext is
  AEAD-authenticated and its digest is signature-bound and independently recomputed. No unbound clear field survived.
- I tested whether the recovery nonce came from the service. It is explicitly 32 fresh client bytes per request and
  the negative rejects an assertion for a nonce the device did not send.
- I tested whether the assertion claims service-independent freshness. The selection explicitly says it does not,
  persists the first view, and labels hostile-service rollback a partition.
- I traced the two non-service arrival paths. Enrollment gains a real transcript binding in this commit; package
  anchoring is deferred and later fails the full re-wrap counterexample.
- I searched for exact-commit/tip application or relay integration. The selected envelope/head service remains
  specification and generated evidence rather than runtime behavior.
