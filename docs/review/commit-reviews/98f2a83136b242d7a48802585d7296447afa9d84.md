# Commit `98f2a83` — `Test every AAD field alone, since a paired negative tests neither`

- Exact generators and published vectors for journal records, content records/key envelopes, device-lock seals,
  and recovery envelopes at `98f2a83:{scripts/generate-*-vectors.mjs,test/vectors/*.json}`.
- `98f2a83:docs/decisions.md` and `:docs/progress.md`, plus the five selection records and TRD AAD contracts.
- Downstream mechanization in `f58e0dea2188e1dd663275165467f7acab46f39c`.

## Change seen while moving backward

Returning to the parent removes thirteen isolated AAD negatives spanning five constructions and restores an audit
rule that exists only as an unfinished progress note. This commit makes each declared AAD field observable through
a negative that keeps the correct key and changes only that field, gives label negatives plausible cross-
construction substitutions, and adds published-versus-executed reconciliation to the journal and recovery files.

## Original-contract trace

This strengthens `OC-022`, `OC-025`, `OC-027`, `OC-038`, `OC-042`, and `OC-047`: clear metadata that selects a
principal, workspace, generation, carrier, purpose, or recovery object must be authenticated, and the evidence must
be capable of detecting omission of each binding rather than only detecting some mismatch in a compound negative.

## Construction and state-machine effect

No production construction changes. Generator helper functions gain test-only label overrides, and the record
generator gains a second test-only envelope purpose. These overrides let the correct key/ciphertext be opened with
exactly one alternate AAD field:

- journal: label and workspace join existing generation and storage-key checks;
- content record: the label joins isolated principal, workspace, generation, sequence, kind, and version checks;
- key envelope: all six fields—label, principal, workspace, version, generation, and purpose—gain checks;
- device-lock seal: its label joins existing correct-key principal, workspace, version, factor, and credential-id
  checks; and
- recovery envelope: label and principal join existing version and public-key checks.

The committed vectors publish those cases. Journal and recovery now also fail when published and executed negative
names differ; record and device lock already had that reconciliation.

## Assumptions and guarantee boundaries

- An isolated open failure shows that the exercised generator includes that field in AES-GCM/XChaCha AAD. It does
  not prove a future production serializer uses the same bytes.
- Correct-key negatives are essential where a field is also in HKDF info; changing both key and AAD cannot locate
  which binding caused rejection.
- A plausible alternate label/purpose tests cross-construction separation better than an arbitrary nonsense byte
  string, but both remain project-selected test cases rather than an external construction oracle.
- The commit's manual remove-one-field audit is not reproducible from committed automation and cannot prevent a
  later unenumerated field. The commit states this limit explicitly.

## Evidence and oracle independence

Static enumeration confirms that each of the 27 declared AAD fields across the five constructions has at least one
correct-key isolated negative after this commit, with journal's two label cases counting as alternatives for one
field. The record, device-lock, and recovery generators reproduced their exact committed vectors and rejected all
published negatives under Node 26.7.0 from an archive of the exact SHA. The journal generator could not run in this
workspace because the locked `@noble/ciphers` package is absent; its field cases and reconciliation were inspected
statically.

Producer and checker still share AAD-building helpers and project-owned field enumeration. This commit therefore
proves the examples against the chosen construction but not completeness of the choice. `f58e0de` later turns the
remove-one-declared-field procedure into a build check, while retaining the declared-field boundary.

## Downstream propagation

All five selection records, TRD §15's vector status, and the generated JSON evidence inherit the stronger negative
shape. The next mechanization commit discovers the same generators by glob, instruments their declared AAD fields,
and preserves the exact manual negatives. Stage implementations can use these vectors as conformance targets but
still must show they serialize identically.

## Consequences and residual failures

The commit closes real false-positive evidence: a field present in HKDF and AAD, or a whole-AAD cross-carrier swap,
could previously leave a missing AAD binding undetected. In particular, key-envelope `purpose` now exercises the
substitution it exists to prevent, and every construction exercises domain separation.

The residual is durability rather than a present missing case. A new field or construction can be omitted from both
the producer's declared list and the checks, and this commit's manual audit would not fail CI. The later mechanized
audit materially reduces future per-field drift but does not create an independent protocol-field registry.

## Falsification attempt and counterevidence

- I enumerated every AAD helper parameter and matched it to an isolated negative that changes only that parameter
  under the original correct key. No declared field is missing at this SHA.
- I traced paired derivation/AAD cases for workspace, principal, and factor; their new `aad-only-*` variants retain
  the original key as claimed.
- I compared the key-envelope and device-lock layouts. They really are six length-prefixed fields with three common
  positions, making label and purpose checks meaningful confusion cases.
- I tested whether a published vector entry could be silently unexecuted. Each generator now reconciles both name
  sets, although two already did so before this commit.
- I looked for an automated remove-one-field oracle at this SHA and found none; the decision record acknowledges
  exactly that limitation.

## Nuance preservation

The change does not claim that any cryptographic construction was broken. It distinguishes defects in evidence
shape from defects in the selected bytes, separates derivation binding from AAD binding, and records that its audit
is not yet durable. Later history preserves that distinction and mechanizes the stated follow-on.

## Verdict

`supported-with-qualifications`: the thirteen cases accurately repair the declared coverage gaps, and the runnable
generators support three of four exact files in this environment. Completeness is still self-declared and manual at
this SHA; `f58e0de` is the later evidence that mechanizes the intended mutation oracle.

## Findings and unresolved checks

- No candidate finding survives for the manual-audit limitation because the commit states it and `f58e0de`
  directly mechanizes it before the pinned tip.
- Stage implementation conformance still needs an oracle that compares production bytes with the vector bytes;
  the generators alone cannot prove the application uses their helpers or complete field list.
