## Original-contract trace

The commit answers `OC-063` and `OC-065`, concretizes the authenticated persistence shape in `OC-024`, and attempts
the vector gate in `OC-039`/`OC-082`. The record nonce ceiling also becomes an enforcement precondition for the
random-IV collision bound, and suite versions implement fail-closed agility rather than negotiable algorithms.

## Construction and state-machine effect

Suite 1 assigns AES-256-GCM to async content/key/network envelopes, XChaCha20-Poly1305 to the synchronous journal,
HKDF-SHA-256 to subkeys, Ed25519 to mutations, HPKE base with X25519/HKDF-SHA256/AES-256-GCM to public-key wraps, and
SHA-256 to hashing. Carrier versions choose the whole suite; unknown versions are reported/refused intact. Content
records bind label/workspace/store/version/generation/sequence/kind in length-prefixed AAD; local key envelopes bind
their corresponding clear fields. Random 96-bit nonces require rotation before the next store `seq` crosses `2^32`,
and `seq` never resets at trim.

## Assumptions and guarantee boundaries

- Suite migration is carrier-version migration, not per-record negotiation; authenticated versions prevent a field
  edit from choosing a weaker suite.
- Random AES-GCM nonces are safe only under the explicit per-generation invocation ceiling.
- Two tabs make a naively derived deterministic nonce unsafe, motivating random nonces plus the checkable counter.
- `L_local` is represented as raw bytes only inside vectors; production must use a non-extractable structured-cloned
  WebCrypto key.
- HPKE identifiers are selected, but provider and executable vector remain 3b work; Ed25519 availability likewise
  lacks a written browser support floor at this SHA.

## Evidence and oracle independence

Published primitive KATs are copied from external specifications and verified by WebCrypto. Record vectors
round-trip and reject wrong workspace/store/generation/sequence/kind/version, tag changes, and truncation. Exact
generators reproduce. Later B-03 work proves several initial AAD negatives were not isolated and adds mutation
coverage, so those later corrections are not credited here. More seriously, `checkSeparation()` does not enforce its
declared stronger rule: `path: 'both'` roles are omitted from both exact-match buckets.

## Downstream propagation

Row 3 is reused byte-for-byte by device-lock custody and later network envelopes. Row 1 becomes the registry extended
by later selections and package generators. `dbfd01f` folds both rows into TRD but loses the numeric record ceiling
and overstates HPKE vector closure. The pinned tip retains the same `both` path and separation logic, while later HPKE
implementation and isolated AAD mutation tests close other decision-time gaps.

## Consequences and residual failures

The constructions give future Stage 1 code precise carriers, authenticated context, migration refusal, and an
enforceable nonce bound. The surviving failure is evidentiary and semantic: CI says no primitive crosses paths while
silently excluding the only role labeled `both`. A future primitive can evade the declared invariant by using that
label, and the current SHA-256 role makes it unclear whether the stronger rule or the data is intended.

## Falsification attempt and counterevidence

I ran both exact generators and inspected every suite role against the separation buckets. Self-consistency is green,
but `both` fails the plain set-membership counterexample. I also tried to turn the missing HPKE vector into a defect of
this commit; the selection explicitly refuses a transcribed, unexecutable KAT and assigns it to 3b, which is honest
scope rather than false evidence here. Record field tampering is broadly covered, and later mutation work fixes the
non-isolated negative design without changing the selected format.

## Nuance preservation

At decision time the record is careful about nonce ceiling, non-extractable custody, multi-tab behavior, and exact
carrier refusal. The suite is careful about deferred provider/support evidence. The separation prose alone is not
faithfully implemented: it claims every role is bucketed and even lists SHA-256 in async, while the data says `both`
and the code ignores it.

## Verdict

`reopen`: the suite and record formats are strong enough to retain, but row 1's claimed build-failing separation
invariant must be made executable or narrowed. The linked row-3 ceiling must also be preserved when this selection is
treated as normative input downstream.
