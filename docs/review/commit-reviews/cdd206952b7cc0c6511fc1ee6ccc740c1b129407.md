## Change seen while moving backward

Returning to `dba43e6` makes row 16 conditional/open again, removes the twelve-month floor and expiry rule,
restores stale inventory pointers to already-selected rows 5 and 7, restores an accepted-set “log” that row 6
had replaced with a mark, and removes the build check that reconciles duration anchors and unselected cells.

## Original-contract trace

The decision closes OC-021/OC-078: if lapse exists, one stated duration covers both resume and hosted
total-device-loss recovery, while local writing remains independent. Updating inventory cells also serves
OC-038's bounded purpose/retention/deletion register and OC-082's requirement that evidence match each selected
construction.

## Construction and state-machine effect

This is policy state rather than a wire format. The clock begins when hosted synchronization stops after grace;
the hosted copy becomes read-only while devices remain writable; ciphertext, required wraps, and the sealed
recovery private key remain available; and at the applicable expiry the service deletes ciphertext and wraps
while retaining the public/sealed recovery material needed for reseeding from a survivor. The commit correctly
keeps deletion execution and partial-failure behavior as future as-built tests.

## Assumptions and guarantee boundaries

It assumes some plan can lapse, but does not select price or grace duration. Twelve months is explicitly a
product judgment, not a measurement. “Floor” governs the minimum promise; “ceiling” governs deletion at the
duration actually stated for a plan. The prose permits a plan to offer longer than twelve months, so the exact
per-plan ceiling must be made explicit at deployment; the repository check only enforces the twelve-month base
anchors. The service's inability to decrypt reduces confidentiality exposure but does not erase storage,
metadata, legal, or operational costs.

## Evidence and oracle independence

`check-retention.mjs` reads five PRD/TRD anchors, recognizes specified duration spellings, rejects conflicting
hosted-retention sentences, and checks that every `unselected` inventory cell names either an open §15 row or a
deployment choice. It is a consistency oracle over prose, not evidence that expiry deletion, backups, or recovery
works. Its parser openly admits it can miss an unrecognized duration phrasing, while anchor presence catches
deletion of a required statement. The choice of twelve months has no independent empirical oracle.

## Downstream propagation

The duration and clock reach PRD §6.10/questions, TRD §§13.8, 15, and 16, the selection/decision/progress record,
package check wiring, and the end-state E-15 model. The audit also repairs acceptance, checkpoint, metrics, log,
backup, and support-material inventory descriptions inherited from earlier rows.

## Consequences and residual failures

The decision makes the hosted recovery deadline plannable and prevents the service from treating retained bytes
as indefinite. After expiry, a user with no surviving replica/archive permanently loses content even if account
and recovery credentials survive. Devices may diverge throughout lapse. Actual deletion across primary stores,
replicas, and backups remains unimplemented evidence; a regex-consistent document does not prove it.

## Falsification attempt and counterevidence

I tested whether separate resume/recovery windows save material storage; because both need the ciphertext and
the wraps are small, a shorter recovery window creates unrecoverable retained bytes without meaningful savings.
I also tested whether “twelve months as floor and ceiling” forbids longer plans. A consistent reading exists only
if twelve months is the global minimum and each plan's disclosed duration becomes its own deletion ceiling; that
deployment rule is not what the current fixed-anchor check verifies. Finally, changing the actual number would
be caught in recognized anchors, but euphemistic or dynamically configured durations require as-built review.

## Nuance preservation

The original conditionality is narrowed rather than erased: price stays open and only the existence of lapse is
taken. The commit preserves local writability, the distinct archive path, and the difference between resume and
recovery. It correctly downgrades the twelve-month rationale from measurement to judgment.

## Verdict

Supported with qualifications. The selection is coherent as a product policy and the inventory reconciliation
repairs real stale state. It is not evidence that twelve months is optimal, and its floor/ceiling semantics,
backup deletion, and actual service execution require explicit deployment and lifecycle tests.
