## R-01. The service accepts positions that no receiver can admit, and an accepted inadmissible position halts every receiver of the workspace permanently with no reason

- **Severity:** medium (latent: the shipped sender cannot produce such a position; a later sender change or a non-conforming member client can, and the damage is permanent and outside the client's reach)
- **Status:** new
- **Release relevance:** fix before the first long-lived service ledger is written. The check is a few lines on the service; after deployment the only repairs are a receiver-side limit increase shipped to every device or an edit of the service ledger.
- **Where:** `account-protocol/submission.ts:46,84` (the only size the service checks on a submission is the 4 MiB envelope wire limit), `account-service/delivery.ts:270-283` (`verifyWire` adds no bound), `src/crdt/accountInbound.ts:165-168` and `:257` (what every receiver admits), `src/crdt/accountReceive.ts:566-583` (only expiry is transient; every other `InboundError` is rethrown), `src/crdt/accountReceiveDriver.ts:149-153` and `src/crdt/accountSyncDriver.ts:206-209` (a thrown step becomes the terminal halt `operation-failed`)

```ts
// account-protocol/submission.ts:84 — the service's only size check on a submission
if (length > ENVELOPE_WIRE_LIMIT) throw Error('Envelope size'); // 4 MiB + 2048, up to 256 updates
// src/crdt/accountInbound.ts:165-168 — what every receiver admits
const fits =
  body.updates.length <= INBOUND_UPDATE_LIMIT && // 255
  body.updates.length + 1 <= MAX_BATCH_RECORDS &&
  size <= INBOUND_UPDATE_BYTES; // 512 KiB decoded; :257 also refuses wire above 512 KiB
if (!fits) fail('size');
```

The receiver's bounds are the documented contract (`docs/account-inbound.md:116`, `docs/account-receive.md:43-44`, `docs/account-crypto/account-ledger.md:137-140`): one entry per page, envelope wire at most 512 KiB, at most 255 original updates, at most 512 KiB decoded. The service enforces none of them. It authenticates and stores any submission whose envelope wire fits in 4 MiB and whose update count fits the codec's 256, and it serves that position to every reader, including the publishing device reading its own ledger back. A reader that meets such a position refuses it with `InboundError('size')`; the receive owner rethrows everything except expiry, the scheduler records `operation-failed`, and the status shows `blocked` with no reason code and no diagnostic entry (`recordPrivateReceiveExpiry` is the only observation the receive owner emits). Reopening the workspace repeats the refusal, because the position stays in the service ledger ahead of every later one.

Demonstrated at the tip with the author's service fixture (`test/account-service-delivery/fixtures.mjs`, `test/account-service-inbound/oracle.mjs`; probe outside `test/`): a submission whose single update is 614,400 bytes is accepted (`kind: 'decision'`) and served back in a read page as a 614,698-byte envelope, above both the receiver's 512 KiB wire bound and its 512 KiB decoded bound; a 16 KiB update is accepted and admissible; a 4 MiB update is refused (`invalid-request`). The receiver's refusal of the 614,698-byte entry is the constant comparison at `src/crdt/accountInbound.ts:257`, read, not executed.

Today the sender stays inside the bounds by construction rather than by any check: local commands are sliced at 16 KiB (`src/crdt/admissionQueue.ts:459-461`, `:495-503`), the new batch stops at 32 originals and 64 KiB (`src/crdt/spoolRecord.ts:25-26`), and nothing before `sealNetworkEnvelope` compares the wire against the receiver's limits. The three sets of constants live in `account-protocol/envelope.ts`, `src/crdt/spoolRecord.ts` and `src/crdt/inboundRecord.ts` and reference each other nowhere. The batching commit is the first time the per-position size policy changed; the ledger contract (`account-ledger.md` §8) states the 64 KiB bound as if it were enforced end to end. **Fix:** refuse at `decideSubmission` any envelope whose wire exceeds `INBOUND_ENVELOPE_LIMIT` (the service sees only ciphertext, and a 512 KiB wire bound also bounds the decoded bytes; the update count sits inside the ciphertext, so its bound stays with the sender); make the codec's 256-update limit and the receiver's 255 agree; assert the receiver's three bounds before `sealNetworkEnvelope`; and turn a refused accepted position into a visible reason and a diagnostic entry instead of the anonymous `operation-failed`.
