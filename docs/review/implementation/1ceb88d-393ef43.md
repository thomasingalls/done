## P-01. The composed owner re-verifies the entire inbound ledger on every operation: about 45 full verifications per edit, 8 per idle poll every five seconds, growing with history

- **Severity:** high (`83eed0b` and `6398e33` make this the production path; F-07/N-01/N-02 change from a deferred constant into continuous load and a hard liveness cliff)

Consequences:

1. **Idle load.** At 400 positions the eight verifications of one empty poll cost about 18 s of carrier time per five-second interval in Node terms; the carrier queue never drains, and journal flushes, captures and the next send pass all wait behind it. N-03 showed one task blocking the queue for up to ten seconds; the poll makes the queue permanently busy.
2. **Edit latency.** In the native probe one two-character edit took 2 to 6 s to be accepted by the service and read back at ten positions, 13 to 15 s at forty and 30 to 32 s at eighty (load average 6 to 7 at the last checkpoint). The growth is linear in history because every pass re-verifies everything; the composed native campaigns never exceed nine positions (`docs/account-conformance-runs/2026-09-11-account-sync/campaign/generated-native.json`), so no test observed it.
3. **The cliff.** The request window is 30 s (`accountReceive.ts:181`); three full verifications, one `verifyDelivery` and the page verification must finish inside it (`accountReceive.ts:244-306`, expiry checked at `:321`). At the measured slope that is roughly 1,500 positions in Node and fewer in the browser. Past it every page is refused as expired, `driveReceiveStep` propagates the `AdmissionError`, the scheduler records `halt = 'operation-failed'` (`accountReceiveDriver.ts:148-154`), and `retry()` halts again. Receiving stops permanently for that workspace.
4. **Positions are cheap to create.** Every accepted input job becomes one capture, one source and one network position, whatever the typing cadence: 30 single-character inserts typed 40 ms apart (1.9 s of typing) produced 30 positions, and 30 typed 300 ms apart produced 30 more (`listCaptures()` reported 33 then 63 captures with one source each). The first burst took 146 s to be accepted and read back (positions 3 to 33); the second took 421 s (positions 33 to 63); at 80 positions a further burst of 30 characters had not settled after 600 s and the probe timed out. A workspace therefore accumulates one position per character typed, and every verification above grows with each keystroke.

The cost is structural: verification results are never cached across loads, `state()` verifies twice per call, `beforeCommit` hooks verify the whole ledger to check one prefix, and the composed `run` wrapper adds a full pass in front of every delivery operation. The deferred cost work (F-07) is now on the critical path of the slice `docs/progress.md` names next, because the server, the poll loop and the composed owner all exist.

**Fix:** cache the verified ledger state keyed by the spool witness (the witness already authenticates the complete record set, so a load whose witness equals the last verified witness needs no re-verification); make `state()` verify once; have `beforeCommit` compare the witness instead of re-verifying; keep only the page verification inside the request window. Any one of these removes most of the multiplier; the first removes the growth.
