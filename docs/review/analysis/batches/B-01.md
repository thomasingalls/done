## Method

disappears on returning to its parent. Trace the change to the original contract and downstream consumers, test a
counterexample, and distinguish project-owned evidence from an independent oracle.

### Cross-amendment chain

The rows close in a coherent dependency order when replayed forward, even though the primary review ran backward:

1. `b9ff071` answers row 12 by making the account credential asymmetric and anchoring its public key in a
   service-signed slot binding. It amends row 1 with ES256, records row 13's slot-verifier PRF role as unused, and
   supplies the ceremony row 14 later reuses.
2. `4259822` answers row 15 with a transition id that binds kind/lineage/epoch/target/payload, plus a signed outcome
   that is the only instruction allowed to discard or apply a pending key transition. It narrows revocation to
   content written by devices after they observe the commit.
3. `5bd9552` answers row 17 with a versioned profile whose exclusions and deviations are explicit. It treats ASVS
   as a floor and names local-first addenda, but only one addendum has a present check and that check overclaims its
   URL-form coverage.
4. `83fe22c` and `449db30` repair row 1's downstream state: first the shipping provider, then stale deferrals. They
   change no primitive or protocol carrier.
5. `cdd2069` answers row 16 as a policy judgment and repairs inventory consumers of rows 5–7/15. Its check proves
   prose/register consistency, not deletion execution.
6. `ff0b48f` can then bind recovery replacement to row 15's id and authenticate it with row 12's credential. The
   fresh nonce closes the replay defect and closes the register scope for review 3b.

This sequence preserves the original separation among account proof, service authorization, content access, and
recovery. It also shows why “closed” cannot mean “implemented”: at the tip the app/relay are still predecessor
surfaces, the transition and nonce services do not exist, most vectors are project-owned composition oracles, and
the booked reviewers' results are absent.

### Guarantee and evidence boundary

- **Cryptographic/design support:** transition-kind binding, nonce freshness, signed outcome authorization,
  service-signed slot-key anchoring, and the stated old-generation/revocation residuals are internally coherent and
  survive the counterexamples attempted in their cards.
- **Policy support:** twelve months is an explicit, bounded product choice, not an empirically optimal duration.
  ASVS 5.0.0 plus named deviations/addenda is a defensible profile shape, not proof those controls pass.
- **Executable evidence:** field-elision checks improve isolation, and register/retention checks catch copied-state
  drift. Except for official primitive/standard inputs, generators and verifiers still largely share project models.
- **Absent evidence:** no exact-tip service implements nonce retirement, transition atomicity, hosted expiry,
  account WebAuthn ceremonies, or hostile-relay lifecycle tests. No external review result exists in the lineage.

## Affirmative conclusions

- The row-15 three-way transition state is the batch's strongest conclusion: unsigned/timed-out responses remain
  silence, a matching signed outcome applies, and a differing signed outcome rebuilds. This is the correct
  fail-closed direction for a transition that can discard a live key.
- Row 14's later nonce and transition-id binding directly repair a replayable deterministic challenge and prevent
  cross-kind redirection without duplicating the target key as a second source of truth.
- Row 12 correctly reframes offline-guessing resistance as absence of a stored secret and identifies public-key
  substitution—not password work factor—as the load-bearing local-record threat.
- Row 16 preserves local writability and distinguishes a policy deadline from protocol-position retentions; its
  inventory reconciliation catches genuine stale concepts rather than merely formatting drift.
- Row 17's explicit exclusions, deviation, and local-first addendum are more informative than an unqualified ASVS
  level. The official source supports the selected stable version and machine-readable basis.
- All six traceability commits are exactly attribution-only. `83fe22c` is more accurately classified as propagation
  than substantive because no algorithm, vector byte, or state transition changes.

## Disconfirmed hypotheses and null results

- I tested whether any traceability commit smuggled a substantive edit; every one is a one-file, one-token
  `_this one_` → preceding-SHA replacement.
- I tested whether `83fe22c` changed the journal primitive or vectors; it only propagates the already-selected WASM
  provider and audit question.
- I tested whether `449db30` independently proves suite completeness; it only rejects known deferrals that outlive
  their owner row, and is correctly bounded as a targeted propagation guard.
- I tested whether shorter independent recovery retention saves meaningful storage; both paths need the same
  ciphertext, so deleting small wraps first produces inaccessible retained bytes rather than useful savings.
- I tested whether timeout/rebuild improves transition liveness safely; it recreates the forged-refusal attack and
  can keep an excluded credential readable, so indefinite authenticated pending is the honest failure mode.
- I searched exact-tip non-document application paths for a current third-party runtime resource. None survived;
  the origin-scan concern is a false assurance gate, not a present remote dependency.
- I tested whether origin and RP-ID checks subsume WebAuthn `crossOrigin`/`topOrigin`; the W3C algorithm treats them
  as separate relying-party policy and explicit iframe delegation makes the distinction reachable.
