## Method

For each commit, inspect the exact object and parent in reverse lineage order, identify what
disappears in the parent, reconstruct what was knowable at that point, trace the conclusion to the
original contract and downstream consumers, challenge its evidence and state machine, and preserve
later amendments as later evidence rather than retroactive justification. Exact archived generators
were run where dependency-free; state-machine and evidence claims were also challenged with mutation,
threshold, replay, stale-profile, and provenance counterexamples.

### The first normative fold

`dbfd01f` correctly carries most load-bearing conclusions from rows 1–3, 13, and the storage half of 14.
Carrier versions select whole suites rather than negotiating algorithms; journal random nonces require no
crash-persistent counter; clear record/envelope fields are authenticated; record `seq` survives trim; a generated
125-bit code makes password stretching unnecessary; PRF is additive; device-lock custody is local rather than
account/recovery authority; a custody record exists at genesis; history is not rekeyed; row-14 step-up stays in 3b.

Three caveats do not survive intact. First, row 13 deliberately separates the atomic custody transaction from
ordinary forward rotation. A locked slot can persist `rotation_owed` if multi-device rotation cannot be invoked
locally, in which case copy must say protection takes full effect at the next rotation. The PRD/TRD instead say
enrollment rotates and promise “from now on” without exposing the pending state. Second, row 3 makes its random
AES-GCM nonce bound executable with a precise branch: before the next `seq` would cross `2^32`, rotate the generation
before writing. The fold preserves only an unnamed budget and non-reset rule. Third, row 1 explicitly says HPKE has
no implementation/vector until 3b, but the fold closes row 20 for all five and says each has a generated,
self-verifying vector. Later HPKE work closes that evidence gap; it does not make the decision-time status accurate.

### Storage constructions and their evidence

The row-13 construction survives adversarial enrollment ordering. Staging factor seals and resealed generation
envelopes while `L_local` remains usable, then swapping/deleting in one transaction, avoids the bricked state. A
generated code rather than a user password resists offline profile guessing; PRF is an optional second seal, not a
root that can strand unsupported platforms. Rotation is necessary because an old profile copy already holds `K_g`
and otherwise reads future ciphertext. The selection itself honestly records the unresolved Stage-4 rotation joint.
Its exact archived generator reproduces row-3 custody envelopes and row-14 encoding under Node 26.7.0.

The row-14 storage selection is also sound with an explicit invariant. Masking each independent random byte by 31
gives every Crockford symbol exactly eight preimages; 25 symbols therefore retain 125 bits. Normalization increases
accepted spellings, not the candidate space. At `10^12` guesses/second exhaustive search remains about
`1.35×10^18` years, so Argon2/PBKDF adds device-dependent cost without repairing an entropy deficit. The exact
generator checks all 256 inputs, RFC 7748 keypair consistency, seals, normalization, and failures. Its network-facing
“single-use” challenge is a pure function of the operation and replayable at this SHA, but is marked draft/3b and
later `ff0b48f` supplies a fresh retired nonce plus transition id and ceremony negatives. That later correction is
not needed to justify the storage entropy choice.

Rows 1 and 3 define strong carrier and storage formats. Published primitive KATs are externally sourced; unknown
carrier versions refuse/report intact; version and every trusted clear record/envelope field enter length-prefixed
AAD; random record nonces use a checkable conservative sequence budget. Both exact generators reproduce. Later B-03
work isolates each AAD negative and adds field-elision mutation, so it strengthens rather than rewrites these formats.
One suite oracle defect persists: SHA-256 is labeled `path: 'both'`, but `checkSeparation()` buckets only exact
`async` and exact `sync` values. The selection says every role is bucketed and the stronger no-shared-primitive rule
is build-failing, while `both` silently enters neither set. Either the role label/rule is wrong or the oracle is.

The journal primitive and construction are well motivated. A 192-bit random nonce removes crash-recoverable state;
the XChaCha draft estimates a `2^-32` collision probability only after roughly `2^80` messages. HKDF occurs once at
open, while per-edit sealing remains synchronous; clear generation and storage slot are authenticated. The selection
also distinguishes initial cipher-only timings from the future whole-write/hardware budget and discloses that the
selected Noble release is newer than its independent audit. The isolated original archive cannot execute without
its locked `@noble/ciphers` installation, so that run is recorded blocked rather than replaced with tip dependencies.
Static inspection finds a surviving oracle gap: the “rotated-generation” case changes `gen` but all cases derive from
the same global `K_g`, so it cannot test the required generation-key lookup.

## Affirmative conclusions

- A 192-bit random journal nonce is the right crash-safe shape: it removes a state rollback point rather than
  relying on journal volume, and the framing/AAD bind every trusted clear field.
- The content-record and local-key-envelope formats are precise, fail closed on unknown versions, and use a
  conservative monotonic sequence to make random AES-GCM nonce safety enforceable.
- Generated 125-bit recovery/device-lock codes justify no stretching only while the full-generation invariant
  holds. Crockford normalization does not reduce their entropy.
- Device-lock factors correctly replace promptless custody without becoming signing, account, or recovery power;
  atomic reseal/delete ordering avoids bricking and forward rotation is necessary against stale profile copies.
- Four dependency-free exact generators—suite, record, recovery, and device lock—reproduce under Node 26.7.0.
- The initial AAD formats survive later isolated-negative and build-time mutation strengthening; those commits fix
  evidence scope rather than changing the selected bytes.
- The plaintext harness captures a meaningful fixed-corpus before-state, exposes profile/method/animation limits,
  and records comfortable headroom for all covered budget concepts.
- The terminology migration fully separates unfiled membership repair, crash replay, and account recovery.
- Review booking is not review completion; the planning commit preserves that distinction and the repository later
  completes the internally dated selection input package.

## Disconfirmed hypotheses and null results

- Most of the first requirements fold is faithful: no-stretch, PRF optionality, custody boundaries, genesis record,
  forward-only rotation, non-resetting `seq`, carrier agility, and the 3a/3b split all survive.
- The device-lock selection itself does not hide the rotation joint; it persists `rotation_owed` and supplies the
  correct alternate copy. The defect begins in propagation, not construction.
- No bias appears in the recovery-code generator: all 32 symbols have exactly eight byte preimages, and permissive
  human normalization does not reduce generated entropy.
- The recovery storage conclusion does not depend on the defective first step-up draft. That draft is explicitly
  network scope and later receives a nonce/transition correction before protocol closure.
- The record and suite generators reproduce byte-for-byte at their exact commit. Initial AAD isolation limitations
  are later corrected without changing selected formats.
- The journal random-nonce collision arithmetic and generation-specific KDF rationale are sound; the surviving
  defect is the rotated-key oracle fixture, not XChaCha framing or primitive KAT.
- The latency harness does not misrepresent throttled emulation as hardware and does not hide animated navigation's
  over-budget settled value. Provenance, not reported arithmetic, is the lasting issue.
- The booking commit never claims a completed review, and the terminology commit leaves no executable old-name
  residue in the exact tree.
