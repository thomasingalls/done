### AAD evidence correction

The parent evidence often changed two causes at once. In particular, changing `workspace_id`,
`principal_id`, or `factor` and re-deriving a key could reject even if the AAD silently omitted that
field; swapping entire AAD layouts could reject on any structural difference while leaving the domain
label or `purpose` unused. `98f2a83` repairs that shape manually. Across journal records, content
records, key envelopes, device-lock seals, and recovery envelopes, every one of 27 declared fields now
has a correct-key negative that changes that field alone. The key envelope gains all six, and every
construction exercises a plausible alternate domain label.

The historical archive reproduced record, device-lock, and recovery vectors and their negative lists
under Node `v26.7.0`; journal could not load because this workspace lacks the locked `@noble/ciphers`.
Static enumeration confirms its label, workspace, generation, and storage-key cases. `f58e0de` then
instruments the generators and enumerates them by glob so CI removes each declared field from seal and
open in turn; all four generator checks pass in the later exact archive when dependencies are present.
This is materially stronger evidence than example negatives alone.

The remaining boundary is explicit: generator and mutation oracle share the project's construction and
declared field enumeration. They show that no **declared** field can be elided without making its
isolated negative open, but cannot detect a protocol field omitted from both construction and inventory,
nor prove a future application serializer uses the generated bytes.

### Measurement correction and decision chain

`66a7c6a` makes the essential correction from primitive speed to the synchronous component: nonce,
seal, framing/base64, and localStorage. Its browser harness is reproducible in shape and accurately
separates means from honest unthrottled p95, but repeatedly replaces one warmed fixed slot. The specified
production journal appends distinct immutable records that accumulate until commit, so new-key insertion,
growth/quota, key construction, and burst position remain unmeasured.

`3670591` runs that component on one Pixel 9a. The arithmetic and thermal/order checks are sound; the
1,930 microsecond 4 kB p95 really breaches the 1 ms budget and directly falsifies scalar desktop/4x-
throttle extrapolation. The common-size pass is bounded to that device/session, and raw samples plus exact
OS/browser state are ignored rather than committed. It does not cure the fixed-slot mismatch.

`138a5f8` then compares two pure-JS XChaCha implementations and libsodium WASM on the same device. Noble
and stablelib differ by only about 0.8% at 4 kB, while libsodium is about 24.2 times faster; this rules out
a cheap import swap on that hardware and supports investigating WASM without changing the wire format.
The 15/44/274 microsecond full-write projections are arithmetically correct but inherit a fixed non-cipher
remainder and means rather than a newly measured p95. Its recorded 37 ms readiness value is only when the
page queries readiness after fetch and agreement work, not the promise resolution timestamp.

`6b021c4` correctly computes 43.7 kB bundle headroom, shows a 149.2 kB gzipped libsodium import is about
3.4 times too large, and says a 24.3 kB inlined narrow-nonce Rust/WASM proxy would leave 19.4 kB. The proxy
is explicitly category pricing rather than the selected XChaCha construction, but its package/version,
harness, command, and result artifact are absent. Later `9b49b74` supplies actual purpose-built bytes and
paired performance; that strongly supports feasibility at the pinned tip but cannot be retrocredited to
the earlier decision's evidence record.

### Platform floor and working-document propagation

`bd87655` usefully selects Baseline newly available for general web features and correctly records that
aggregate `web-cryptography`/`webauthn` status cannot resolve algorithms or extensions. Its dated Ed25519
table is consistent with compatibility data, it keeps the Stage-4 conformance test open, and optional PRF
cannot lock users out because the generated code remains the root.

Two inferences do not follow. Browser feature interoperability supplies no lower bound on CPU/storage
latency and therefore cannot substitute for the journal budget's hardware population. The supposedly
complete capability-specific inventory also omits WebCrypto X25519 even though suite 1 selects it for
HPKE and the contemporaneous row-14 record explicitly calls it the same load-bearing Stage-4 question.
Current compatibility data makes immediate incompatibility unlikely; the defect is the missing dated
policy/test/absence path.

`ffd90e3` accurately carries the measured selection state into working documents. Its seven rows are six
plaintext measurement rows plus the journal specification row; “selection stands, budget does not” is
awkward shorthand, but the same prose and TRD preserve the actual decision—retain the selection and
budget while Stage 1 fixes or consciously revises the 4 kB case. No independent contradiction survives.

## Affirmative conclusions

- Correct-key, one-field-at-a-time negatives repair the prior paired-cause blind spot for all 27 declared
  fields across five AAD constructions; build-time elision makes that inventory durable across all
  discovered generators.
- Domain labels now exercise plausible cross-construction substitutions, and key-envelope `purpose`
  exercises the credential/envelope confusion it exists to prevent.
- Measuring nonce, seal, framing/base64, and storage is the correct journal unit. One-device evidence
  decisively falsifies the desktop-throttle extrapolation and establishes a 4 kB breach on the recorded
  Pixel 9a.
- On that phone and locked versions, stablelib does not materially improve over Noble, while WASM has
  large compute margin. The wire construction need not change merely to pursue that performance fix.
- The recorded libsodium bundle is outside the then-current headroom. A later actual purpose-built module
  supports category feasibility, subject to B-02's assurance/integration qualifications.
- Baseline newly available is a coherent feature-interoperability policy; Ed25519 clears its dated floor,
  and generated-code-root recovery correctly makes PRF absence a convenience loss rather than lockout.
- Working-document catch-up preserves the selection, budget, breach, and named Stage-1 obligation rather
  than silently declaring the empirical defect closed.

## Disconfirmed hypotheses and null results

- Every declared AAD field has an isolated correct-key negative after `98f2a83`; no present field-
  coverage omission survived static enumeration or the runnable historical checks.
- The manual audit's lack of durability does not survive as a tip finding: `f58e0de` mechanizes the
  declared-field elision and glob-discovers all four composition generators.
- Published measurement arithmetic—budget ratios, JS/WASM ratios, headroom, and projected remainders—
  is internally consistent. Findings concern provenance, scope, or workload fidelity rather than math.
- Stablelib is not a practical pure-JS performance escape on the measured Pixel, and the narrow-nonce
  candidate is never misrepresented as wire-compatible or selected.
- The real-device run is genuine counterevidence to scalar desktop throttling; lack of raw samples does
  not negate the observed 4 kB breach.
- The support-floor commit openly preserves Ed25519 conformance debt and PRF optionality. No hidden PRF
  mandatory branch or fallback contradiction survived.
- The maintenance commit changes no normative construction and accurately accounts for its seven rows.
