## Prospective protocol

The decision is whether the selected combination has a bounded history/scratch lifetime under the
specified operations, and preserves the existing Account composition controls. This is a bounded
qualification experiment, not a claim of constant total memory, secure memory erasure, handset
behavior or a whole-release pass. Writing and undo history legitimately grow during editing.

Run the existing eleven owned files selected by receive-editor, sync, editing, handoff, capture and
admission fixtures, with their exact current assertions, source inputs and deadlines. This covers
119 dispositions, including generated sync, receive/editor ordering, source capture, close/drain,
crash recovery and Account refusal boundaries. Preserve every failure. No whole-wrapper obligation
is resolved by these direct-file runs.

For lifetime measurements use disposable Chromium pages and the real workspace/admission owners
with a controlled in-memory carrier. Keep those component observations distinct from the native
storage fixtures above. Use four fresh sessions in reference/candidate/candidate/reference order;
the candidate is explicit `local-snapshots`. Each performs 12 cycles of 40 text edits, with
structural/metadata/remote/undo/redo resets and repeated close/reopen. Also test pressure, discard,
retry and a close waiting for an owned database write. Synthetic writing and fixed operator identity
only; no personal browser or installed application.

A temporary exact-anchor observation transform records WeakRefs and scalar lifecycle counts for
workspace histories and comparison scratch. It must not keep a strong object inventory or change
ownership/verification. Preserve original and observed source hashes. Observe the installed pinned
Yjs listener map, destruction events, live document/history counts and cleared scratch fields.
Use external CDP garbage collection between separate browser tasks: at most five collections with
an event-loop boundary before deciding that a reference remains. An uncollected object needs
investigation, not automatic classification as a leak. Repeat post-close observations while keeping
the closed workspace facade reachable; verify that the observation mechanism itself releases objects.

Hard invariants: at quiescent eligible text boundaries there are at most two live workspace histories
(one accepted and one spare), and at reset boundaries at most one. Idle fencing does not accumulate
listeners. Every created history document is destroyed after its owner closes, and no history or
document remains reachable through the retained closed facade after collection. Exercise large
then small comparison workloads, exception cleanup and reentry: at most two scratch encoders are
retained, each no more than 256 KiB of byte backing storage and 1,024 cleared string slots. Every
retained byte/string field must be cleared. Fault controls deliberately retain history, omit listener
removal and leave comparison fields uncleared; each must fail its designated observation.

Heap usage and externally allocated/backing-store bytes are secondary descriptive measurements.
Keep all per-session/per-cycle readings and writing/undo-size context. Do not subtract quantiles or
claim a causal heap saving from four sessions. Measurements within each session are dependent;
sessions share one host and pinned engine. Four sessions test repeatability and opposite execution
order, not a powered population estimate. Instrumentation, forced GC, synthetic carriers and short
workloads limit external validity. Native controls supplement these observations rather than turning
them into application-memory or power-loss evidence.

A failure blocks this phase pending a bounded repair and renewed affected checks. An instrument
setup error is retained separately and may be repaired before the fixed campaign; semantic failures
are never erased by reruns. Positive results permit the following unchanged application gate and
Local activation decision, not automatic activation. Larger rewrites and broader eligibility stay out.

pressure/discard/retry and close waiting for an owned write. Candidate sessions create/destroy 36
workspace histories each versus 508 for the reference. Five closed facade objects remain reachable

batches actually use two histories with one idle fence; reference uses one. All three deliberate
faults are caught. Large-input scratch reaches about 1.36 MB/1,601 fields before cleanup; retained
scratch is cleared and stays within its fixed caps. Process/browser teardown completes.

The comparator is the same integrated code in reference preparation mode. These are controlled
in-memory carrier observations, supplemented by separate native storage/Account fixtures. Heap
usage increases about 1.8 MB in both modes; this does not establish a general heap saving.
