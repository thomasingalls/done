# Phase 539 — let a full-check device hold routine sync so reduction can run

Local reduction is the way out of the full reference check, and it could not run on the devices that needed it. Three things stood in its way, all read from the released source `759890ca0`: a running attempt was cancelled by the first storage job of any routine receive poll; an attempt could not start while a sync lane was busy, and the hint it waits for was withdrawn during each validation with nothing announcing its return; and a prepared but unaccepted position made the cut ineligible. Phase 309 had already recorded the first of these.

A device whose adopted ledger view has no continuation indexes now reports that as a scheduling hint (`src/crdt/ledgerHints.ts`). On such a device a due reduction attempt may ask routine sync to hold. The sender finishes any position it has already allocated and then parks before allocating another, which is the settled cut reduction requires. Once an attempt starts, receive passes are deferred as well; hints that arrive meanwhile are kept, not dropped. The hold is a hint read live by the scheduler and grants nothing: every eligibility, authority, journal and commit check stays in the owner. A device that is not on the full-check path never holds, so its scheduling is unchanged.

The hold is bounded. It lasts at most five minutes from the moment sync is quiet under it, then yields sixty seconds of ordinary sync before it may engage again. Capacity and missing-history refusals end it until a manual retry or reopen; an ineligible cut releases it; a hidden page, storage pressure, a halt or disposal release it. Both intervals are scheduling bounds chosen without measurement.

The change was drafted in a separate seat on `claude/reduction-full-check-hold` from `e9ec1e697` and was not committed there. Its seven-file patch applied here unchanged. Review after integration found one defect, repaired here: the five-minute budget started only when an attempt began, so a sender that had parked while pending input kept the attempt from starting stayed parked without limit. Two added cases fail on the draft as received and pass now.

Nineteen unit cases in three new files cover the hint, the scheduler and the reduction owner. The three files are registered as reviewed Vitest selections, taking the case denominator from 2,629 to 2,648 and the planning inventory from 1,105 to 1,108 obligations with 552 still unresolved. The runtime census gains one source and records one domain site moving twelve lines in `accountReceive.ts`; the construction registry's line follows it. No changed line contains a domain label or cryptographic call.

Typecheck and the ten CI contract checks pass. On the finished tree the full unit suite passes with the CI command, `pnpm test --maxWorkers=1`: 338 files, 3,265 cases, one existing skip, 271.34 s. The attempt before it is retained as a failure: two cases in `test/workspaceAdmission.test.ts` failed with the host's load average near 11, one by timing out at 5,187 ms against its 5,000 ms limit and one on the assertion that a discarded private history is disposed. That file imports no module changed here and passed alone three times (238 cases). The cause of the assertion failure was not investigated. No deadline was changed.

Limits. No browser suite, timing or device run. A device that has already reduced is not on the full-check path, so a later reduction there can still be cancelled by routine polls, as the operator observed on the phone on 2026-11-11. While the hold is parked, local edits are saved but not sent, for up to five minutes, and the sync notice shows the sender as idle. No deployment.
