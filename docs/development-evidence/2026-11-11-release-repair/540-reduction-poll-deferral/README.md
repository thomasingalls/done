# Phase 540 — a running reduction skips routine idle receive polls

On an ordinary device a reduction attempt was cancelled by the receive lane's own idle timer. The lane polls every 15 to 20 seconds with a healthy notification stream and every 5 seconds without one. The poll's first storage job replaces the preparation token in `accountCapture.ts`, and the attempt's next step then stops as interrupted. The usable window was about the poll interval minus the ten-second quiet clock, so a reduction that needed longer never finished while online. Phase 309 recorded it, and the operator saw it on a production phone on 2026-11-11: maintenance began reading, stopped partway, and the Receiving lane turned to working at the same moment. Phase 539 addressed this only for a device on the full-check path.

While an attempt runs, and through a saved slice that continues at once, the reduction owner now publishes a third, lighter hold tier that asks the receive lane to skip only the pass whose sole cause is the idle timer. The scheduler marks a receive deadline as routine only when it came from a clean idle sweep. A content or control hint, reconnection, an authority change, a retry, an unanswered submit, or a pass that made progress or was marked dirty all clear the mark, so real incoming writing is received at once and still interrupts the attempt as before. This tier never parks the sender.

The deferral lasts at most sixty seconds per run, counted from the run's first slice, and is then followed by the existing sixty-second yield before it may engage again. Any wait between slices releases it, as do a hidden page, storage pressure, an inactive carrier or workspace, a halt, disposal and every final outcome. On a full-check device the phase 539 hold takes precedence and is unchanged. Sixty seconds is an unmeasured scheduling bound; the only timed whole reduction, in phase 309, took 36 seconds on a lab desktop.

Two alternatives were rejected. Exempting routine polls from replacing the preparation token would make their storage job wait behind a preparation slice, which breaks the phase 83 rule that ordinary queued storage never waits behind read-only preparation. Deferring every receive pass would delay real incoming writing with no evidence that it is needed.

The change was drafted by a delegated agent in this checkout and reviewed here before registration. Twelve cases are added to the two existing hold test files; nine fail on the phase 539 behaviour and three guard unchanged behaviour. No existing assertion changed. The reviewer's rerun of the five neighbouring files passes 69 cases, and the typecheck and the five account-sync source-anchor controls pass. The ten CI contract checks pass, and on the finished tree the full unit suite passes with the CI command, `pnpm test --maxWorkers=1`: 338 files, 3,277 cases, one existing skip, 272.55 s. The case denominator moves from 2,648 to 2,660 with the planning inventory unchanged at 1,108 obligations and 552 unresolved. The runtime census is refreshed after review: no changed line contains a domain label or cryptographic call, and one registered site moves one line with the construction registry following it.

Limits. No browser, native or device run and no timing. It is not known whether sixty seconds covers a reduced phone's suffix replays, which have no memo. A peer that publishes continuously still interrupts every attempt through its hints. An incoming edit that only a poll would find arrives up to sixty seconds late during a reduction. This phase does not help a device whose reduction is refused at a size limit: on 2026-11-11 the operator's desktop stopped at "Reading saved history…", which is the preparation budget, not starvation. No deployment.
