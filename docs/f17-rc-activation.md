## Measurements and limits of inference

This is an existing engineering acceptance benchmark on one host, not a randomized treatment
comparison. The exposure is the selected Local factory mode; observations are repeated actions/keys
within each project session, and decisions use the existing per-project measures. The primary
quantity is the recorder's trusted-key-to-painted-frame p95 for card typing; compose, startup,
authenticated replay, focus and navigation retain their existing guardrails. All samples and
measurement notes remain. Dependencies within sessions, JIT/GC, frame scheduling and host drift
preclude treating 120 keys as independent users or attributing a difference from old runs entirely
to this patch. There is no power calculation or fleet confidence interval for this fixed gate.

CPU-4x is the retained emulation reference, not a Pixel measurement or a hardware population.
The earlier physical companion remains only a rough planning comparison. The current fixture also
uses its existing widened scheduling viewport; preserve its recorded viewport notes. Measurements
cover the prescribed workload, not arbitrary history size, handset/native IME, whole-profile memory,
secure erasure or loaded-host capacity. The short lifetime observations and canonical closed-facade
retention limitation remain as previously recorded.
