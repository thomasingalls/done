Useful children aren’t buried under “sort this out.” Every item and note stays in source order. Tap a linked example to reveal its place in A, without opening a keyboard.

The tradeoff: A reduces visible writing by hiding whole branches; you decide what to open. B reduces indentation and exposes nested lines, but doesn’t magically shorten the list. It is a temporary reading projection, with one action back to the document—not a second list to curate.

Shared behavior: current day stays explicit; Next keeps its three-card admission limit. Copy is the initial mode. Move removes only the highlighted source subtree after acceptance. Both are one undoable action. These controls could accompany either scanning view.
