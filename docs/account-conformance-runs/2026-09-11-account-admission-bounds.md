## Result and evidence

The immutable v1 reproduction accepts 32,767 raw descriptor bytes and refuses 32,768 in both the
manifest and SemanticAdmission. Generated Yjs histories with 12,000 writer incarnations or separated
deletions produce 41,618-byte SV and 39,750-byte DS respectively, from checkpoints below 216 kB.
These are capacity counterexamples, not a claim that all delete sets grow monotonically.

Both signatures now use explicit v2 domains/version fields and separate SHA-256 hashes of decoded
opaque SV/DS bytes. Transport still retains all raw bytes. Client replay must agree three ways;
the content-blind service cannot establish their semantic truth. The combined 16 MiB raw-pair bound
fits the existing 72 MiB completion/receipt limit. Checkpoint encryption/AAD remains v1, with the
same ciphertext vector as before. New owners refuse completed v1 artifacts without rewriting them.
