## Why the first reduction can be substantial

`planLocalRetention` first decrypts/validates the exact original inventory and calls
`replayFullLocalHistory` to construct the reference. With no predecessor baseline, it calls
`prepareLocalBaseline`, which runs the full history again before summarising. Candidate construction
independently replays its compact result; the planner replays the exact proposed survivors and
compares them with the reference. Some of this is intended independence, rather than redundant I/O.

The final phase retains fresh authority and exact inventory comparisons, durable nonce reservation,
atomic replacement and readback. `contentStore.prepareHistoryRemoval` then calls `load`, which
validates the reduced baseline, and calls `verifyCompleteLocalBaseline` again on the retained
candidate. Both paths stay intact. No deletion is selected by age/range, no unproven candidate is
installed, and pending source/undo/history/authority checks are not omitted by this experiment.

The existing owner metrics separate read-only preparation from the exclusive final phase. On this
small desktop fixture, preparation is51ms and the final fence48.4ms, including adapter8.7ms,
readback0.7ms, reload17.1ms and explicit candidate replay9.6ms. Those timings include observer/fixture
cost, are single exploratory values, and are not an explanation or estimate for a10,000-row phone.
The raw report retains all metrics, including cumulative pre-observation work; only retention-specific
costs have one call and belong exclusively to this attempt. No linear scaling claim is made.

## Waiting, interruption and already implemented improvements

Automatic starting requires1,024 rows, a visible/durable owner and ten user-quiet seconds. Input,
lock, visibility and lifecycle can defer/cancel preparation. The policy's60-second retry applies to
transient refusals; stable capacity/history/ineligible refusals park until a new authenticated hint.
A reopen also has to finish startup recovery before a live maintenance owner exists. The expensive
first opening and first conversion are distinct costs, so maintenance cannot retroactively shorten
that initial protected opening.

The production core verification functions match dc53fecf, but development phases83–84 already add
queue-priority interruption of read-only preparation and wait for foreground sync readiness without
restarting user quiet on routine polling. These changes are not deployed or confirmed on this phone.
The phase119 status snapshots do not reveal how many original attempts ran, where they yielded,
when the atomic exchange occurred, or which phase occupied the wait. We cannot claim the phone was
continuously calculating, deadlocked, or waiting on any particular network operation. Its initial
wall-time attribution remains open;120 establishes only that reduction eventually completed.
