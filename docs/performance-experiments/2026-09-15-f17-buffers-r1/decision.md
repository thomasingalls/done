# Preserve the buffer prototype; retain R3 plus repair eligibility

The corrected prototype establishes actual two-history reuse in focused tests and in every ordinary browser measurement. It retains detached history/selection state, exact predecessor checks, normalization fallback, full catch-up comparison and existing full post-admission verification. All224 affected tests pass, including 60 new generated histories;121 ownership/workspace cases repeat after the reference-lifetime correction. Three deliberate faults are caught.

The final fixed comparison does not justify selecting this implementation. Ordinary paired medians regress 6.8% early /4.0% later, with 5.0%/4.3% p95 regressions. Every individual ordinary median and p95 pair is slower. The Unicode fallback pair is slower at the median by 3.2%/2.1%, with mixed p95. Small session counts and host/JIT/GC variation limit magnitude and attribution claims, but there is no consistent gain to retain. The original four-session observation before the reference-lifetime correction also does not favor the candidate and remains separate.

Keep this incremental patch, its retirement mechanism and all controls as an optional prototype. The selected experimental combination remains R3 preparation reuse + comparison pool + editor shortcut + primitive value-string reuse + membership-repair eligibility. Keep optional publication/UI work separate. This does not reject persistent buffers as an architecture or require one optimization to solve the entire budget gap.

This first variant intentionally copies full undo/redo stacks and adds a complete catch-up comparison before the next edit. Those may consume the saved-fork benefit; no profile here establishes that attribution. The next bounded step should measure the added comparison and stack-copy costs against the saved fork, and specify when delta catch-up establishes exactly the same basis as a fresh fork. A follow-up may then test replacing this prototype's added comparison with a proved invariant while retaining the existing full post-admission comparison. Removing that guard without the proof/control work is not authorized by these timings. Incremental stack mirroring remains another option after measuring its cost.

Wider Unicode eligibility, native ownership/crash/memory/Account qualification and the unchanged application gate remain ahead of selection for RC/default use. Incremental verification of the existing canonical check remains a distinct later proof and failure-detection-policy decision. No release count, budget, default, product-data or deployment change follows.
