# Phase24: isolate request-local sharing and publication reuse

## Question and decision
Which of the two independent changes contributes to the held candidate's publication cadence and displayed receiver backlog under the fixed H6/M2/Q2 workload? Isolate before changing runtime or flow control. This is a bounded diagnostic experiment, not a release or performance promotion; phase23 remains held regardless of diagnostic success.

## Design, units, estimands and precision
A four-condition 2x2 component isolation: A=21(neither), B=22(request-local only), C=21+23 publication adapter only, D=23(both). Retain byte-exact native inputs and independently verify C composition. Fresh whole journey/state/profiles is assignment, analysis and decision unit; dependent per-key/request/progress records are observations, never independent samples. Fixed prospective A,B,D,C,C,D,B,A gives each condition two mirrored observations and checks consistency/period drift; not randomized, not a population or interaction-significance estimate. Descriptive within-order contrasts B-A/D-C for request-local sharing and C-A/D-B for publication reuse; report all eight values and any inconsistency, no pooled per-request p-values. Two journeys per condition cannot estimate stable tail distributions or capacity in general; enough only to choose the next trace. Prior held23 remains the acceptance record.

## Identification and limits
Reset copied native state and actual seeded browser profiles for every journey; cold service and fresh process-owned proof caches. Fixed client and offered workload means changed publication counts/coalescing are outcomes, not a controlled publication schedule. This identifies component effects only for these finite diagnostic journeys assuming comparable host load and no carryover. Reversed order exposes but cannot eliminate nonlinear drift. A diagnostic censor changes later history and cannot clear the original heavy600s gate. Observer and browser overhead are common but may interact with scheduling; instrumented follow-up is a separate phase after24 closure. No production/WAN/phone/population or exhaustive security claim.
